# FingerWorks demo video — script outline

**The premise:** This script was unfinished. So it gets finished on camera —
by editing this very document, hands-free, using FingerWorks. The "task" is
real: reading back, fixing, and finishing an actual draft, not a staged demo
task. ~75–90 seconds. Every feature earns its place because the edit
genuinely needs it.

**Framing:** Screen recording of a text editor with this draft open, plus a
small picture-in-picture of the presenter's hand (or `--debug`'s live camera
window with the skeleton overlay — genuinely useful here, not just a debug
tool: it's the clearest possible proof this is really gesture-driven).
Captions burned in for each gesture the first time it's used.

---

## 0:00–0:10 — Hook

Cold open. A text editor fills the screen: **`demo_video_script.md`**, a
visibly half-written draft, cursor blinking mid-sentence. A hand rises
toward a webcam clipped to the monitor. No mouse or keyboard touched.

> **On-screen text:** "This script wasn't finished. So we finished it —
> like this."

## 0:10–0:15 — Setup line

> "Watch the whole thing get edited, start to finish, hands-free."

Straight into the edit — no further preamble.

## 0:15–0:50 — The edit (feature walkthrough)

Each beat is a real edit to the document, not a gesture shown for its own
sake.

1. **Scroll to find the spot** — point the **left** index finger up to
   scroll up through the draft to the half-finished paragraph; thumb-down
   to scroll back and re-check something above it.
2. **Zoom to reread** — the draft has a typo too small to read comfortably
   at this resolution. Open left hand (five fingers) to zoom the screen in
   on that line; closed fist to zoom back out once it's spotted.
3. **Click + drag to select** — right hand: pinch thumb+index, hold, and
   drag across the typo'd phrase to select it. Green click indicator
   flashes at the cursor on the initial pinch — quick, satisfying payoff
   shot.
4. **Keyboard toggle** — closed right-hand fist brings up the on-screen
   keyboard, right over the selected text.
5. **Fix the typo** — type the correction: a capital letter (Shift), a
   backspace to fix a fumbled key, an em dash or parenthesis from the
   `123` symbols page. This is the "it's a real keyboard" beat.
6. **Drag the keyboard out of the way** — it's covering the next line
   that also needs a fix. Pinch-hold-drag the panel's grip strip to slide
   it aside without closing it. Motivated by an actual need, not shown in
   isolation.
7. **Paste a missing line** — a sentence was drafted elsewhere (e.g.
   visible copied a moment earlier in the same recording, or implied).
   Left-hand scissors (index+middle) pastes it straight in — no keyboard
   needed for it.
8. **Cut a redundant sentence** — right-hand scissors on a line that's
   now duplicated after the paste. Shown as a quick "oh, that's redundant
   now" beat, not a dry feature callout.
9. **Close the keyboard, click Save** — fist to close the keyboard,
   pinch to click **Save**. Yellow indicator flash on a right-click
   somewhere in this stretch too, just to show both colors exist.

## 0:50–1:00 — The pause beat

Someone knocks, off-frame. Thumb+pinky pose — the control bar's status
dot goes orange, "Paused," visibly mid-edit. A beat passes; **Resume** is
clicked on the control bar itself (proving it's reachable without a
gesture, for exactly this reason) rather than needing to re-pose.

> **Caption:** "Step away without losing your place."

## 1:00–1:12 — The small stuff, fast

Quick montage, captions only, no narration:

- Settings opens; **Cursor snappiness** slider dragged live, cursor
  visibly gets snappier for the last stretch of editing. *("Tune it to
  your hand.")*
- Control bar dragged to the opposite corner mid-edit, out of the way of
  the paragraph it was overlapping. *("Move it wherever helps.")*

## 1:12–1:20 — The reveal

Cut to the finished document — cursor at the bottom, draft now complete,
saved. A beat. Then:

> **On-screen text:** "You just watched us write this script. With this
> app. Hands-free."

Pull back to a wide shot mirroring the opening: hands still nowhere near
the mouse or keyboard.

> **Voiceover:** "Same hands the whole time. Never touched a thing."
> **End card:** FingerWorks — logo/name, one-line tagline, where to get it.

---

## Notes for whoever shoots this

- **This only works if the edits are real.** Script the *before* draft
  deliberately (an actual half-finished version of this file, typos and
  all) so what's typed/fixed on camera is genuinely fixing something, not
  miming it. Faked "editing" reads instantly as staged and kills the joke.
- **Don't narrate every gesture out loud** — captions for the first
  instance of each gesture are enough. Save spoken lines for the hook,
  the pause beat, and the reveal.
- **Keep `--debug`'s live camera window on screen** (small, corner) for
  the whole demo if it doesn't clutter the shot.
- **One continuous take if possible** (or invisible cuts) — the moment it
  cuts away from the hands, it stops proving anything.
- **The reveal only lands if the audience didn't clock the premise
  early.** Don't title-card "we're editing our own script" up front —
  let the half-finished-draft framing register as just the demo content
  until the 1:12 reveal recontextualizes the whole video.
